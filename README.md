I’m not that old, and right now I’m totally fine living literally in the middle of the city, feeling its energy, the noise, all that buzzing life. But watching older people, and watching myself too, I can tell the urge to move a bit further out is slowly creeping up on me. To spend my free time not at loud events but, say, driving out to the forest and just being in the quiet. Same with where I live. Now I look at quiet, residential neighborhoods, houses with backyards that have a BBQ area, [gas powered outdoor ovens](https://www.fontanaforniusa.com/collections/gas-pizza-ovens), a place to relax, and I realize this stuff gets closer to me with every year. And I feel more like working now so I can make that dream happen later.
